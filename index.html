Marshmallow math

The recipe says three envelopes, your drawer has gold sheets, and the pan is never the size the recipe assumed. Marshmallow math converts powder to sheets with labeled weights, sizes a batch to the pan you actually own, and counts the pieces plus the dusting mix before you cut. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Gelatin converter - powder grams become envelopes and bronze, silver, gold or platinum sheets.
  • Pan fill - pan dimensions and slab height become the batch size and ingredient split.
  • Cut yield - pan and piece size become the grid, the count and the dusting mix.

Labeled norms

Envelope 7 g; sheet weights bronze 3.3, silver 2.5, gold 2.0, platinum 1.7 g (bloom strengths vary by brand). Slab density about 0.5 g per ml. Base batch by mass: sugar 300, corn syrup 330, water 240, gelatin 21 per 891 g. Dusting 2 g per piece, 50/50 cornstarch and powdered sugar. Labeled kitchen references, not rules - your recipe and your labels win.
